Departments

  • Department of Computer Science
    •

    The Department of Computer Science focuses on developing students' knowledge of computers, programming, software development, and modern technologies. It provides practical and theoretical knowledge in areas such as Python, Java, web development, databases, and artificial intelligence. The department prepares students for careers in the IT industry.

  • Department of Commerce
    •

    The Department of Commerce offers courses in accounting, finance, business management, and economics. It equips students with the skills needed for careers in business, finance, and entrepreneurship. The department emphasizes practical applications of commerce principles.

  • Department of Mathematics
    •

    The Department of Mathematics provides a strong foundation in mathematical concepts, problem-solving, and analytical thinking. It covers topics such as algebra, calculus, statistics, and discrete mathematics. The department prepares students for careers in research, education, and various industries that require mathematical expertise

  • Department of English
    •

    The Department of English aims to improve students' communication, reading, writing, and language skills. It introduces students to English literature, grammar, poetry, prose, and creative writing. The department helps students develop confidence and effective communication skills.

  • Department of Science
    •

    The Department of Science provides knowledge of scientific principles and discoveries. It focuses on subjects such as Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and other scientific fields. The department encourages practical learning, experimentation, research, and scientific thinking.

  • Department of Sanskrit
    •

    The Department of Sanskrit focuses on teaching the Sanskrit language, literature, grammar, and ancient Indian knowledge. It introduces students to classical texts, poetry, philosophy, and cultural traditions. The department helps preserve and promote India's rich linguistic and cultural heritage.
