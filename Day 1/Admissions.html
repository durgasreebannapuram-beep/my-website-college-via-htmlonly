Admissions

We welcome applications from students who are passionate about learning and eager to contribute to our vibrant academic community. Our admissions process is designed to identify talented individuals who will thrive in our supportive and challenging environment.

Admission Process

  1. Visit the college or official admission portal.
  2. Check the available courses.
  3. Fill out the application form.
  4. Submit the required documents.
  5. Complete the admission process.

Required Documents

  • 10th Class Marks Memo
  • Intermediate Marks Memo
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Passport Size Photographs
